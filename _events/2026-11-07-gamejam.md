Join us for GAMEJAM 2026 on the 7th of November from 10.30 onwards.

Join us for GameJam, our first Hackathon of the year! In groups of 2-6, you'll have 24 hours to make a game with the theme Time with the chance to win cash prizes and meet industry judges! GameJam is free to come to and there'll be food provided, but make sure you get a ticket to ensure you get food. Keep an eye on our Instagram and Discord for more info! Can't wait to see you all!

A chance to win over £2000 in prizes as well as a potential fast track to interview for an internship! Come and show off your game development skills!
