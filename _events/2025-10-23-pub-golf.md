Join us for CSS X Matrix Halloween Pub Golf! Dress code is not mandatory but highly encouraged, can’t wait to see you
all there👻

# RULES:

- Teams of 4-6
- Elect a team captain, whose job it is to handle their team & make sure a ref is present for all drinking
- The score for each team is determined by the average number of sips for the team

## Scoring

- Best team name: -2
- Best dressed as a team : -3
- Best dressed as a team runner up: -2
- Drinking without a ref present: +20
- Vomiting : +5
- Toilet break between pubs: +1
- Late to next pub: +2
- “what hole are we on?” : +1
- Spilled drink: +3
- Fall over: +5
- Buying food mid-course: +2
- Lying about sips: +5

## PARS

If you finish your drink in the par your score is 0, but if you exceed the par your score is the number of sips you
take.

- Beer: 1
- Mixed drink: 1
- Wine: 1
- Pitcher: 2

## ROUTE & TASKS

### Steam

- pilsner (par 2)
- non-alcoholic: pint of soft drink in 1 sip + swap left & right shoes until next hole
  +10 if you fail

### W.G. Grace

- 175ml house red
- guest ale (highest percentage possible)
- non-alcoholic: 2 glasses of soft drink with ketchup, mustard & mango chutney
  1 sip for each drink
  +10 if you fail

### Den & Terrace

- beer of your choice that you must feed to your teammate
- non-alcoholic: let someone on a different team draw somthing on your face, bite out of a lemon wedge, sprite in 1 sip
  +10 if you fail

Water Hazard (going to the toilet +2 points)

Extra challenge: do 20 push-ups outside in front of a ref for -2 points (1 person per team)

### The Berkeley

- 2 pitchers per team
- only 2 people per pitcher
- guinness - first sip doesn’t count as a sip if you split the g
- non-alcoholic: stand & do 10 star jumps every 5 minutes, mocktail in 1 sip
  extra challenge: each vodka/tequila shot is worth -1 point
  Water Hazard (going to the toilet +2 points)

+10 if you fail

### White Harte

- Old moot
- non-alcoholic: tie shoelaces together & drink a non-alcoholic pitcher of choice in 2 sips

### Daisy’s

Get to daisy’s before midnight for queue skips. Free entry & discounted drinks all night.

The winning team gets VIP cards, which give you queue skips any day except Wednesday, half price entry & 30 percent off
drinks every day.
Best dressed team gets free drinks tokens for Daisy’s.

Best dressed individual gets a vip card

## BARTER SYSTEM

you can challenge another team to finish a task in a certain number of sips
if they agree, the losing team gets +1 point & the winning team gets -1
must have a ref present.

## EXTRA CHALLENGES

- buy a ref a drink: -2
- make a dance tik tok with your whole team (once only): -3
- sing a song of your choice as group for 30 secs (only once) : -2
