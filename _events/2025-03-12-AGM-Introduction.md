## AGM 2025

Come to the 2025 AGM on Thursday 20th March at 6pm in Queens 1.40 to vote for next year's committee members! 

If you would like to be a part of running CSS next year, why not sign up as a nominee? You can pick at most 2 roles to run for. Link and QR on last slide for nominee sign ups. If you want to propose a new motion, use this link: [css-motion-form](https://bit.ly/css-motions-form). Find these links in our bio too! Supported by Bristol SU.
