## A Deep Dive into Verification
In modern processor development, design verification plays a crucial role in ensuring correctness, performance, and power efficiency before silicon fabrication. 

Join us to learn how design verification ensures the reliability of the chips powering billions of devices worldwide!

**Note**: If you're a Year 2 Computer Science student unsure about your Year 3 module choices, consider exploring Design Verification. It’s a valuable option for gaining insight into this critical field, which ensures the reliability and correctness of hardware designs. 

If you're interested in hardware, embedded systems, or chip design, this module could be a great fit!
