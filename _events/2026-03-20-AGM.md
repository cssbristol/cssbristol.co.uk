# Roles

## President

As president, you are responsible for the strategic direction and overall operations of CSS. You chair meetings, oversee
budgeting and event planning, support the committee, and ensure the society delivers value to its members.

Acting as the primary representative of CSS, you will liaise with the university and external partners while maintaining
strong engagement within the department. This role offers significant leadership experience and the opportunity to shape
the culture and impact of the society.

## Vice President

As vice president, you’ll work closely with the president to organise and run the committee smoothly. The main
responsibilities of the role are to secure general sponsorship for the society, to build and maintain relationships with
alumni and to assist in the planning and delivery of events. This position offers a fantastic opportunity to gain
leadership experience and expand your professional network. It’s ideal for someone who enjoys paying attention to finer
details, is organised, and isn't afraid of emails.

## Treasurer

Responsible for managing all of CSS’s finances, paying expenses, and overseeing budgets, including financial
planning and paying for major events such as GameJam, BrisHack, and the Summer BBQ.

## Secretary

Primarily responsible for logistics and admin work such as booking rooms, writing meeting minutes, and
corresponding with key contacts within the university. You will work closely with the President and Vice President, and
have a big-picture overview of how the society operates.

## Social Secretary

Responsible for organising socials every 2ish weeks. Including organising the MVBall, the yearly
Scotland Yard Social, the engineering pub socials and the CS family scheme. Important part is building connections with
other social secs to organise collabs with other societies.

## Press Officer

Responsible for keeping the social media (mainly Instagram sometimes TIk Tok) up to date, responding to
DMs and making up graphics for events like socials, BrisHack & GameJam. Quite laid back, ideal for anyone interested in
graphic design!

## BrisHack Officer

Responsible for the planning of BrisHack for your year of committee. One 24 hour event typically
taking place in the first half of the second term. You will have a counterpart in BEEES and you will be working
alongside them to make the event happen.

## General Officer

Responsible for supporting those who may have a little too much on their plate through taking on a
variety of roles within the society. This role is centred on being a reliable supporter, ensuring that other committee
members can perform to the best of their ability, while also taking initiative whenever needed.

## EDI Officer

As an EDI officer, you help ensure that events are inclusive and promote diversity. Work can be done
alongside the social secretary, where you help advise them in what events would allow for all people from different
backgrounds to be included. Workshops and talks can also be done to help promote diversity.

## Talks Officer

Responsible for organising company and academic talks throughout the year. This includes reaching out to
speakers, managing communication, confirming logistics, and hosting events. You’ll build relationships with industry
professionals, coordinate with other committee members for promotion and room bookings, and ensure talks are relevant
and engaging for students.

## Technical Events Officer

As technical events officer, you will work alongside the Talks Officer to organise a range of computer-science related
events throughout the year. This can include talks, workshops, presentations, competitions, and study sessions. This
involves company outreach, room bookings, and catering. Last year’s technical events included a lightning talks series,
a game jam, and a hackathon.

## Webmaster

Update website, Discord, Whatsapp with events and job posting from sponsors. Work with BrisHack officer to update
BrisHack website. Responsible for email administration.

## Outreach Officer

As Outreach Officer, you work alongside the EDI Officer to engage new and underrepresented communities. You build
connections with schools, societies, and external groups, helping to promote accessibility and inclusion within CSS.
This role focuses on widening participation and ensuring our society reaches beyond its existing membership.
