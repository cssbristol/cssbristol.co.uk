GameJam is one of our annual hackathons.

In teams of up to 6 people, you have 24 hours to make a game that fits the theme.

This year’s theme is Retro!

The games will be judged by industry professionals with cash prizes up for grabs!

You must sign up beforehand by getting a free ticket here!

Dinner and breakfast will be provided.

Schedule:

10:00 - 12:00: Signup and prep

12:00 - 12:00: Code!

12:00 - 16:00: Judging and Results
