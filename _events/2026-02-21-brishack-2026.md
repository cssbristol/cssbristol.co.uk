Join us for BrisHack 2026 on 21st February! Can’t wait to see lots of you there 🪐

# What is BrisHack?

BrisHack is UOB's biggest hackathon, run by CSS and BEEES! In teams of up to 6 people, you have 24 hours to make a
project that fits the theme.

This year's theme is space! Projects will be judged by industry professionals, with cash prizes up for grabs!

If you want to come you must sign up! Tickets are free, and include food!
