Get ready for the ultimate engineering face-off!

Join us for an epic evening of LaserTag as all the top engineering societies — CSS, AeroSOC, BEEES, and MechSoc — battle
it out for glory, bragging rights, and the title of Engineering LaserTag Champions 2025!

Whether you’re a sharpshooter or just here for the fun, this is your chance to team up with your society, take aim, and
show what your discipline is made of. Expect friendly rivalry, fast-paced action, and plenty of laughs.
